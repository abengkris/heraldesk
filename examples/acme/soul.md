# Acme

Acme builds small, fast dashboards for operations teams.

## Voice

Plain, specific and calm. Short sentences. No hype, no em dashes and no generic greetings.

## Always

- Prefer a concrete number or a named trade-off over a vague claim.
- Mention when a feature is still in preview.
- Keep the reader's time.

## Never

- Do not invent customer names or metrics.
- Do not compare competitors by name.
- Do not promise a release date that has not been confirmed.
