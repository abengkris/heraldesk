# Brand brief: Acme

## Audience

Operations leads at small SaaS companies who review dashboards every morning.

## Goals

- Explain one useful workflow per post.
- Build trust with concrete, verifiable details.
- Drive trial signups from the profile link.

## Content pillars

- Faster reporting
- Fewer manual spreadsheets
- Practical operations habits

## Competitors and references

List dashboards and operations tools you respect, and explain what they do well.
